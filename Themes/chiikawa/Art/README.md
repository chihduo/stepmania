The title menu adds two transparent assets: [the twelve-pose character sheet](title-dancers%204x3.png) and [the three stationary cartoon pads](title-dance-pads.png). Four poses for each of Chiikawa, Hachiware and Usagi combine with Lua stepping/hopping into a 3.2-second loop. The sheet aligns cropped source poses without changing their RGBA pixels; the pads retain the complete generated output. [Preview the loop](../../../Docs/media/title-dance.gif). Original drawings, exact prompts and packing records remain under `Docs/theme-preview/chiikawa/title-dance/` on the [development branch](../../../Docs/ChiikawaDevelopment.md).

[The release manifest](../../../Docs/theme-preview/chiikawa/release-manifest.json) records each asset's SHA-256, source-output identifier and characters. Exact prompts, original images, the comparison gallery and retired-candidate history remain on the [development branch](../../../Docs/ChiikawaDevelopment.md).

`menu-tunnel.png` and `menu-particle.png` are unchanged original-theme textures. Lua applies colors and transforms during rendering. Their source files are recorded in the manifest.

These illustrations are AI-generated fan artwork, not official Chiikawa artwork. Fonts, noteskin and judgment graphics are inherited from Classic (`Themes/default`).
